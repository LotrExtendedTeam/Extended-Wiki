# Repair Tomes

Repair tomes are crafting modifier items that when applied to an item in an anvil, changes the result of the repair and is consumed upon repair.

## Smith Tome

::infobox
type: item
title: Tome of the Smith
image: items/smith_tome.png
rarity: Common
tab: Misc
renewable: true
stack: 1
::end-infobox

The Tome of the Smith negates the contained modifier and enchantments cost of the item in the final repair cost.


## Obtaining

All reforge tomes can be found in smithy based structures and as treasure in ruins. Smiths also sell tomes, although different smiths sell them at different prices depending on the faction.

## History

- 1.10.3:

## Trivia

The Tome of the Smith is a response to players noting that repair costs for items are pricy at times (which is usually the fault with having many modifiers applied to the item)

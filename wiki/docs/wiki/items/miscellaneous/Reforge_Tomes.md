# Reforge Tomes

Reforge tomes are crafting modifier items that when applied to an item in an anvil, changes what modifiers can be obtained on Reforging.

Unique to reforge tomes among anvil applicable items is that they not consumed upon reforging.

## Warrior Tome

::infobox
type: item
title: Tome of the Warrior
image: items/combat_tome.png
rarity: Common
tab: Misc
renewable: true
stack: 1
::end-infobox

The Tome of the Warrior guarantees combat based modifiers upon reforging an item.

## Artisan Tome

::infobox
type: item
title: Tome of the Artisan
image: items/artisan_tome.png
rarity: Common
tab: Misc
renewable: true
stack: 1
::end-infobox

The Tome of the Artisan guarantees production/tool based modifiers upon reforging an item.

## Obtaining

All reforge tomes can be found in smithy based structures and as treasure in ruins. Smiths also sell tomes, although different smiths sell them at different prices depending on the faction.

## History

- 1.10.2:
    - Added Tome of the Artisan
    - Added Tome of the Warrior

## Trivia

Reforge Tomes are the solution to the issue where people would reforge a pickaxe and get only combat modifiers. This is how legacy did it, but given how much people wanted combat modifiers to be removed from tools, this was the compromise.
